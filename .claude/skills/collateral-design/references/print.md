# Print

Most collateral never goes to a commercial press — it's a digital PDF, an office/laser print, a screen export. **Reach for this reference only when the piece is genuinely going to a print shop / offset run.**

*When* it is a real press run, print punishes screen habits hardest: RGB color, px units, "close enough" edges, and low DPI all become defects on paper.

## Light First — Printed Black Is the Exception

**Design print light, and make the dark version second.** A near-black printed piece is genuinely rare: it soaks ink, shows every scuff and fingerprint, costs more, and goes muddy on uncoated stock. **Business cards are the notable exception.**

## The Back of a Business Card

Keep it almost empty. The back of a card is **the logo — and at most one creative mark or motif crop.** Nothing else.

## Margin Is the Space *Inside* the Trim

Print inverts the web's vocabulary: **margin is interior**. Be generous, but **scale it to the format**: a letter sheet wants roughly **1/2–3/4in**, while a **business card's content may run right up to the safe line**.

## Work in Real Dimensions, Not Pixels

Set the document in **mm or inches at final trim size** — never px. Common sizes:

- **Business card** — 3.5 × 2 in (US) / 85 × 55 mm (EU)
- **Flyer / sell sheet** — US Letter 8.5 × 11 in / A4 210 × 297 mm
- **Poster** — A3/A2/A1, or 18×24 / 24×36 in
- **Tri-fold brochure** — Letter/A4 folded into three panels

## Bleed, Trim, and Safe Margin

- **Trim** — the final cut line; the finished size.
- **Bleed** — extend any color/image that touches the edge **past** the trim, usually **3 mm / 0.125 in**.
- **Safe margin** — keep all critical content **inside** the trim by ~3–5 mm / 0.125–0.25 in.

**Set the document up at bleed size, not trim size.**

## Color: CMYK, Not RGB

Screens are RGB; print is **CMYK**. Brand colors defined in hex/RGB **shift on press**.

- **Convert to CMYK and check the translation.**
- **Rich black vs. registration black.** For large solid black areas use a rich black (e.g. C40 M30 Y30 K100).
- **Mind ink coverage** on heavy dark designs (total ink limit, typically ~300%).

## Resolution

- **Raster images: 300 DPI at final print size.**
- **Prefer vector** for the logo, type, icons, and any line art.

## Folds, Panels, and Reading Order

A fold **reorders reading.** Design *to* the fold:

- Lay out by panel, in the order the reader encounters them.
- Don't run critical content across a fold line.
- Account for panel-width differences on tuck folds.

## Stock, Finish, and the Physical Object

The paper is part of the design. Note the intended stock/finish in the handoff.

## Ship a Press-Ready PDF

The deliverable is a **print-ready PDF**:

- Correct trim size **with bleed included**.
- **CMYK** color, fonts **embedded or outlined**, images at 300 DPI.
- One final read **at size** — spelling, numbers, phone/URL, alignment.
