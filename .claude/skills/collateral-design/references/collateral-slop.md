# Collateral Slop — the Assembled-Not-Designed Tells

Patterns that immediately signal a piece of collateral was *assembled from a template* rather than *designed*. Run on the **rendered artifact, in its real context**.

## Across All Collateral

- **The template underneath.** You can feel the master: same placement every page.
- **Everything one weight.** No visible hierarchy; nothing lands.
- **Centered by reflex.** Every headline centered; the reading path dies.
- **Accent everywhere.** The reserved color sprinkled on every element.
- **Stock-photo clichés.** The handshake, the diverse-team-at-laptop.
- **Type that dies at final size.** Sizes carried from screen to small physical format.
- **Clipped content.** A headline or stat cut off by the frame edge.
- **An icon-font glyph standing in for the logo.**
- **A rebuilt logo.** Symbol and wordmark assembled by hand instead of placed as the locked asset.
