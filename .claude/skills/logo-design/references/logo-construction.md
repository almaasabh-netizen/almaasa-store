# Logo Construction & Craft

The build-level craft that separates a resolved mark from a rough one.

## Geometric harmony — the golden ratio, corrected by eye

- **Construct with circles and squares in ~1.618 ratios.**
- **Stick to geometry; use the pen tool.**
- **But trust your eyes over the math.** Optical alignment beats numeric alignment.
- **Unify the paths.** Merge into a single uniform shape where the mark is one color.

## Typography & tracking — the wordmark is drawn, not typed

- **Licensed faces only.** Commercial license confirmed.
- **Choose a face with character** appropriate to the emotional target.
- **Be picky about letterform spacing.** Kern individual pairs by hand.
- **The wordmark must read as one unified graphic**, not a row of separate glyphs.

## Lockup ratio — mark to wordmark

- **Balance them optically, not by bounding box.** Match cap-height to mark-height by eye.
- **Define the spacing and alignment** as a rule (x-based clear space).

## Delivery & variants

- **Color mode:** work in RGB.
- **Variants:** full-color, black, white, grayscale, inverted. **Black-and-white does not mean solid 100% black:** opacity steps / tints within a single value are legitimate.
- **App icon:** rounded + square, with and without the name, PNG + SVG.

## The construction check

Before calling a mark done: geometry relates · paths unified · type licensed and hand-tracked · lockup optically balanced · holds in black-and-white · survives the implementation grid.
